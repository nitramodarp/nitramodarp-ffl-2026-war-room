The Recap

Week 1 produced the most lopsided good-team-loses-anyway result the league will probably see all year: Chuba Bucky Doo put up 176.5 points, got a 37.9-point night from Jaxson Dart and 29.2 from Justin Jefferson, and still lost by 37 points because mikelanta dropped 213.65 on them behind a 47.95-point outing from Caleb Williams — the league's top individual score by a wide margin. That mikelanta-Chuba Bucky Doo matchup was, functionally, the championship game nobody scheduled for Week 1. The closest finish of the week belonged to Meredith's Fan Club, who edged Screaming Eagles 99.8 to 99.5, a margin thin enough that a single incompletion somewhere probably decided it. On the other end, Silverbacks handled Pukachu Chews You comfortably, 128.35 to 100.95, aided by a 30.4-point game from David Montgomery. Bench management was the story almost everywhere else. Waffle House sat Lamar Jackson (34.2 points) in favor of Baker Mayfield (14.8) and still somehow won their game — small mercy in an otherwise indefensible call. Pukachu Chews You wasn't so lucky, benching Jordan Love's 32.35 points for Bo Nix's 10.75 in a loss. Dak to the Future got away with sitting Tyler Shough (38.3) behind Dak Prescott (23.15) and won anyway. Silverbacks left 22.4 points of Kyle Monangai on the bench while starting Ja'Marr Chase for 2.2. The worst of the bunch was arguably Joe Mama Angie Daddy, who started the Houston defense (-2.0) over the Seattle defense sitting on their bench (16.0), an 18-point unforced error in a game they still lost by 8.35.

Transaction Desk

On the other side of the ledger, mikelanta's Michael Penix (added, dropped, re-added within the same transaction log) and Isiah Pacheco pickups have produced nothing so far, though none of it mattered given the team already led the league in scoring.
