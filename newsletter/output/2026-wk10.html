The Recap

The week's closest call belonged to Broke Dak Mountain, which held off Non Regulators 146.8 to 140.55, a 6.25-point margin that was the nearest thing to drama the league produced. Christian McCaffrey carried Broke Dak Mountain with 51.3 points, and Josh Allen added 30.3, enough to survive a huge night from Non Regulators' Jayden Daniels (34.25) and Chuba Hubbard (33.7). Non Regulators did it to themselves, too: Alvin Kamara sat on the bench and put up 16.9 points while Chris Rodriguez started and managed 0.5, a 16.4-point gap that turned a winnable game into a loss.

Everything else on the slate was a wipeout by comparison. Joe Mama Angie Daddy demolished Waffle House 221.35 to 108.7, the week's biggest blowout by a wide margin, behind Joe Burrow's 49.25 and George Pickens' 41.0 — with Jahmyr Gibbs (42.7) also going off and Brock Purdy posting 48.55 from the bench that didn't even matter. Silverbacks beat Screaming Eagles 108.65 to 63.4, and demon44 handled mikelanta 143.7 to 97.0, with Long Live King Henry edging Dak to the Future 151.25 to 139.45.

The week's ugliest bench decision belonged to Screaming Eagles, who started Drake Maye (3.5) over Matthew Stafford, who scored 32.25 from the bench — a 28.75-point gap in a loss. mikelanta also left points on the table, starting Geno Smith (7.4) over J.J. McCarthy (26.05), an 18.65-point miss in a loss to demon44. Both of those are now multi-week habits worth watching.

Transaction Desk

The Wednesday-morning waiver run, which cleared right after last week's edition went out, was the week's biggest: eight claims went through. Non Regulators swapped the Giants defense for the Browns, Long Live King Henry added Tank Dell and Konata Mumpfield and dropped the Saints defense, Screaming Eagles added the Buccaneers defense and dropped Jake Ferguson, Silverbacks added Bryce Young and Corey Kiner while dropping Michael Penix Jr. and Adonai Mitchell, demon44 added Audric Estime, and mikelanta swapped the 49ers defense for the Packers.

Meredith's Fan Club ran one of the busiest weeks in the league, cycling through four moves — the Bengals in, the Bengals out for the Colts, then two separate receiver adds in Adonai Mitchell and Chimere Dike — none of it costing any priority since nobody else wanted those players. It's roster churn, not a real swing, but Adonai Mitchell and Chimere Dike are depth additions worth monitoring on a team that just posted 162 points without needing either of them to start big.

Screaming Eagles later used their No. 6 waiver slot to add the 49ers defense after dropping Tampa Bay — a modest move with a modest cost, and the early return (1.0 point) isn't going to move the needle either way. Waffle House, picking first in priority order and also sitting at 1-9, used that position efficiently for once: Drew Lock has scored 26.0 points since being added for nothing, and Josh Downs is sitting on 32.3 cumulative points since his week 7 pickup — actual value coming out of a lost season. Dak to the Future's pickup of Wan'Dale Robinson is listed as a possible handcuff of Carnell Tate, which reads as a sensible speculative stash rather than panic, and cost no priority to acquire.

The quieter story remains Joe Mama Angie Daddy's week 6 claim on Tre Tucker, which has produced 54.9 cumulative points for zero priority spent — one of the better value pickups anyone's made all season, on a team that's already running away with the league.
