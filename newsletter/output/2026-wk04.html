Transaction Desk

The week's one real swing was Meredith's Fan Club burning a premium waiver slot — third in the order — on Oronde Gadsden, a tight end who returned 6.3 points in his first week on the roster. That's a real cost for a modest return so far, and worth tracking given the premium priority spent. Most other moves cost nothing by design. demon44 used its last-place priority slot to grab Bryce Young off waivers, which is about as close to a free swing as this league allows, and it paid off immediately — 30.75 points in his first week aboard. Silverbacks added Michael Penix as an uncontested free agent and got 17.95 points out of him right away, while also picking up Kendre Miller as a likely injury-insurance move behind Travis Etienne, which is a sensible depth add regardless of whether he ever sees the field. Joe Mama Angie Daddy's addition of Sione Vaki reads the same way, a depth pickup behind Jahmyr Gibbs rather than a speculative flier. Chuba Bucky Doo rounded out its bench with Darren Waller and Kalif Raymond, neither of which cost anything and neither of which has produced much yet (5.1 and 1.6 points respectively) — low cost, low reward moves that are fine as organizational depth but shouldn't be mistaken for difference-makers.
