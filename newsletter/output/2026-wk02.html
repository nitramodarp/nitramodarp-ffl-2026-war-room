Friends For Life — Week 2 Recap

The Recap

Dak to the Future and demon44 are the only unbeaten teams left after two weeks, and they got there very differently. Dak to the Future throttled Meredith's Fan Club 138.45 to 106.7 behind Dak Prescott's 44.55 points and a 33.3-point day from CeeDee Lamb, while demon44 needed a 29.2-point Jonathan Taylor game to hold off Silverbacks 115.55 to 98.6. The closest game of the week was a rematch of last week's tight finish: Chuba Bucky Doo beat Long Live King Henry by 1.2 points, 128.4 to 127.2, with Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 43-point outing doing basically all the work. The biggest blowout belonged to Broke Dak Mountain, who ran up 122.5 against Pukachu Chews You's 74.45 — a 48.05-point margin built on Josh Allen's league-best 54.3 points, the top individual score of the week by a wide margin. The bench-mistake column is where this week gets ugly. Every single matchup produced a real, avoidable points-on-the-bench error, and several were not close. Long Live King Henry sat Jared Goff (45.05 points) in favor of Jalen Hurts (26.8), a self-inflicted 18.25-point gap in a game they lost by 1.2 — the margin that actually would have flipped the result. Screaming Eagles benched Matthew Stafford's 43.25 points for Drake Maye's 11.1, a 32.15-point gap, in a game they lost by 20.45. Silverbacks left Bryce Young (35.95) on the bench for Justin Herbert (14.8), a 21.15-point swing, in a loss decided by 16.95. None of those decisions were season-defining on their own, but stacked together it was a week where almost every team out-thought itself. Joe Mama Angie Daddy's win over Screaming Eagles was comfortable at 133.55 to 113.1, though it came with its own bench math: Travis Kelce scored 21.6 points from the bench while Rhamondre Stevenson managed 3.1 as a starter, an 18.5-point gift that didn't end up costing the result. Waffle House beat mikelanta 118.95 to 97.9 on the strength of Davante Adams' 37.5 points, despite starting DJ Moore's -0.1 over Brian Robinson's 7.7 on the bench.

Transaction Desk

The waiver wire was almost entirely a defense-and-depth week — no team spent real priority capital, and nothing here was a bidding war. mikelanta's pickup of Jacoby Brissett cost nothing (they're sitting at the back of the priority order anyway) and has already returned 11.45 points in the one week it's been rostered, which is a fine value proposition for a QB stash that cost literally nothing to acquire. The more interesting note is Pukachu Chews You adding Dylan Sampson, which the data flags as a likely handcuff to Quinshon Judkins already on that roster — a sensible bit of insurance given Judkins is one of their starters, not a speculative flier. Everything else — Long Live King Henry grabbing the Bengals defense, demon44 picking up Kenyon Sadiq, Demarcus Robinson, and the Packers defense, Pukachu Chews You also adding the Chargers defense — came at zero cost, uncontested, and none of it has moved the needle yet beyond replacement-level production. demon44's whole roster-building approach continues to be built on exactly these kinds of free pickups, which is notable mostly because it's now 2-0 doing it.
