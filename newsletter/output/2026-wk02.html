The Recap

Chuba Bucky Doo beat Long Live King Henry 128.4 to 127.2, the second straight week the two Hearts division rivals have decided their game by almost exactly one point. Jaxon Smith-Njigba single-handedly kept Chuba Bucky Doo alive with 43 points, while King Henry's Jared Goff sat on the bench and put up 45.05 — the single most damaging bench decision in the league this week, a 18.25-point gap between Goff and the Jalen Hurts start that replaced him. King Henry has now benched two different 40-plus scorers in as many weeks and has nothing to show for it. The rest of the board wasn't as close.
